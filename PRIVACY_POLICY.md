# PriceSnap – Privacy Policy (Prototype Version)

**Last updated:** 16 September 2026  
**Owner:** Luke Ponga, trading as PriceSnap  
**Contact:** lukeponga9@gmail.com

---

## 1. Introduction
PriceSnap is an experimental prototype that provides AI-generated resale value estimates based on photos submitted by users. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the rights available to you. Because this is a prototype, features and data flows may change as the service evolves.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
PriceSnap collects only the information required to operate the prototype, including photos you upload for appraisal and basic technical information such as device type, browser version, and error logs. No account system is currently implemented, and no personal identifiers beyond your submitted content are collected.

---

## 3. How Your Photos Are Used
When you submit an image, it is transmitted to an AI model hosted by Google AI Studio (Gemini) for analysis. Under Google’s free-tier Gemini API terms, your inputs may be reviewed by humans and may be used to improve Google products and services. Images are used solely to generate appraisal results within the prototype and are not used by PriceSnap for training or advertising.

---

## 4. Data Processing Locations
Because the prototype relies on Google AI Studio and other cloud services, your information may be processed outside New Zealand, including in the United States or other regions where Google operates its infrastructure.

---

## 5. Retention & Deletion
Images submitted for appraisal are deleted from PriceSnap’s prototype environment immediately after analysis and are not stored by PriceSnap. Diagnostic logs may be retained for up to 30 days for troubleshooting. If backups are generated by hosting providers, they may persist for up to 90 days before automatic deletion. You may request deletion of any remaining diagnostic data by contacting the email listed above.

---

## 6. Minimum Age Requirement
PriceSnap is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age, in accordance with Google’s Gemini API terms. By using the prototype, you confirm that you are at least 18.

---

## 7. Analytics & Diagnostics
Anonymous analytics or crash logs may be collected to improve reliability. These logs do not include image content or personal identifiers and are used solely for debugging and performance monitoring.

---

## 8. Sharing Your Information
PriceSnap does not sell your data. Information may be shared only with service providers necessary to operate the prototype, such as Google AI Studio for image analysis and the hosting provider for serving the application. These providers may process data overseas and under their own privacy terms.

---

## 9. Security
The prototype uses secure communication protocols and cloud-hosted infrastructure. However, because this is an early-stage product, security measures may evolve as the service matures. Users should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential images.

---

## 10. Your Rights
You may request deletion of diagnostic logs or other information associated with your use of the prototype by contacting the email above. Because images are deleted immediately after analysis, they cannot be retrieved or removed after processing.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated as PriceSnap transitions from prototype to production. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or website.
